## E-Commerce Revenue Root Cause & Forecasting System

**Python | SQL | Pandas | scikit-learn | Plotly | Streamlit | OpenAI API**

- Built an e-commerce analytics system combining revenue forecasting, product-demand forecasting, anomaly detection, and explainable root-cause analysis across relational and uploaded CSV data.
- Developed time-based ML pipelines using lag, rolling, seasonality, price, transaction, and customer features; compared baseline, Linear Regression, and Gradient Boosting models using MAE, R², and WMAPE.
- Implemented revenue and demand decomposition to quantify the impact of traffic, conversion, AOV, refunds, transactions, units per transaction, and revenue per unit.
- Created an interactive Streamlit dashboard with product-level forecasts, anomaly monitoring, marketing/product drivers, downloadable portfolio forecasts, and optional evidence-grounded AI summaries.
